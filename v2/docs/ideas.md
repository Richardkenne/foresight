## Phases & Modes

1. **"What If" mode** — click a gate, change probability with slider, re-simulate instantly. "What happens if bottleneck goes from 18% to 40%?"
2. **Action Plan** — after simulation, button "What should I do?" extracts 3-5 concrete steps from the graph
3. **Sacred Root overlay** — hover a node, shows which sacred root governs it with the verse
4. **Timeline view** — nodes have `time` field. Show horizontal timeline below graph: when each thing happens
5. **Trap Detector** — highlight bottlenecks by failure %, show "82% die here = business opportunity here"
6. **Multi-person mode** — "me + Ira", "me + cofounder". Two profiles interacting in the same graph
7. **Loop/Retry detection** — fail → learn → retry with better probability. Currently linear, should loop
8. **External shock events** — crisis, pandemic, sudden opportunity. A "shock" node that modifies all downstream probabilities
9. **Generational view** — simulate not just your life but your children's. Today's choices change their probabilities
10. **Emotional state tracker** — each node has emotional cost. After 3 failed bottlenecks, quit probability rises. Burnout as cumulative variable
11. **Resource meter** — money, energy, time as finite resources consumed at each node
12. **Network effect nodes** — results depend on how many people choose the same path. If everyone goes Upwork AI, probability drops for all
13. **Milestone checkpoints** — save progress points in graph. If you fail later, restart from checkpoint not zero
14. **Comparison with real people** — link 140+ School of Hard Knox interviews: "Victor took this path, Myron took that one"
15. **Voice narration** — simulator narrates the path as particles move. Sacred texts read aloud at gates

## Input Methods

16. **URL input** — paste LinkedIn/site/article link → analyze and generate that person/business scenario
17. **CV/Resume upload** — upload PDF → extract skills, experience, gaps → simulate next career step
18. **Screenshot input** — screenshot of tweet, post, financial dashboard → generate scenario from it
19. **Location pin** — pick a city on map → simulate "what happens if I move there" with real data
20. **Financial snapshot** — enter income/expenses → simulate financial scenarios calibrated to real numbers
21. **Conversation input** — paste a chat (WhatsApp, email) → analyze relational dynamics, simulate outcomes
22. **Comparator** — "Me vs [famous person]" — enter name, pull their path, compare with your profile

## Diagnosis

23. **Life audit scan** — 20 questions, simulator maps WHERE you are on every dimension (money, health, relationships, faith, skills), shows which area is silently killing you
24. **Pattern detector** — enter last 5 important decisions → simulator finds your repetitive pattern and tells you which sacred root you're violating every time
25. **Blind spot finder** — simulator shows what you're NOT considering. Every person has 2-3 gates they systematically ignore
26. **Fear Decoder** — describe what blocks you in free text. Simulator maps it to a sacred root: "Your fear isn't 'not ready' — it's SR-009 (pride). You're afraid of failing publicly."
27. **Sacred Root Score** — personal score on each of the 36 sacred roots. Answer 36 questions, get your map: strong in diligence, weak in patience, critical in stewardship. Every simulation calibrates to YOUR sacred profile.
28. **Skill Gap X-Ray** — given desired outcome, simulator calculates exactly which 2-3 skills you're missing to pass the gates. Not 20 courses — the 2 precise things that unlock everything.

## Prevention

29. **Red flag scanner** — describe a situation (business deal, relationship, investment) → simulator shows 3 danger signals that most people ignore before disaster
30. **Regret simulator** — simulate yourself at 70 looking back. Which decisions of today will you regret most? Based on real regret data by age group (Bronnie Ware, Cornell study)
31. **Pre-Mortem** — before starting something, simulator holds the funeral. "It failed. Why?" Forces you to list reasons, maps them to graph gates. Used by CIA and Amazon.
32. **Regret Minimization** — Bezos framework automated. For each decision, simulator projects you at 80 and calculates which choice you'd regret NOT having made most.

## Action

33. **Next best move** — given profile + current situation, what's the SINGLE move with best impact/risk ratio? Not 10 options — one.
34. **Weekly oracle** — every Monday, simulator gives ONE focus for the week based on where you are in your path
35. **Gate Training** — simulator identifies your weakest gate (for you: selling/monetizing). Then gives daily micro-exercises to raise the probability of passing it. Not theory — 15-minute actions.
36. **Opportunity Radar** — based on profile + location + skills, simulator scans: "Here are 3 opportunities that exist NOW for someone exactly like you, that 95% don't see."
37. **Seasonal Strategy** — certain businesses work in certain periods. Simulator maps: "For Upwork AI automation, Q1 is best for proposals because corporate budgets are fresh. You're losing the window."

## Relationships

38. **Relationship X-Ray** — enter a relationship (business partner, friend, romantic partner). Simulator maps sacred roots of both, shows where values collide. Predicts the breaking point.
39. **Second Order Effects** — simulator shows what happens to OTHERS. "If I go freelance" → effect on Ira, family, network. Cascades of consequences.
40. **Conversation simulator** — "I need to ask my boss for a raise" → simulate 5 different ways to say it and success probability of each

## Accountability & Tracking

41. **Decision Journal** — every simulation saved. In 6 months review: "I simulated X, chose Y, Z happened." Simulator learns from your real results and calibrates future probabilities on you.
42. **Accountability Loop** — choose a path, set a deadline. Simulator sends weekly check: "You're at gate 3, should be at gate 5. You're 2 weeks behind." Based on real average timelines.
43. **Commitment Device** — choose a path, stake something real. "If I don't send 5 proposals by Friday, simulator donates $20 from my wallet to a cause I hate." Based on Stickk.com/behavioral economics.
44. **Momentum Tracker** — physics of success: object in motion stays in motion. Measures momentum: how many actions in last 7 days? Positive momentum = gate probabilities rise. Zero actions for 3 days = probabilities crash.
45. **Debt of Inaction** — every week simulator calculates debt accumulated for NOT acting. Not in money — in position. "You're 14 weeks behind where you'd be if you started Upwork when you first simulated it."
46. **Anti-Portfolio** — every time you DON'T do something (don't invest, don't send proposal, don't move), simulator tracks it. After 1 year shows: "These are things you didn't do and how much they cost you."
47. **Exit Interview** — end of day, 60 seconds: "What did you do today that moves you toward outcome-good?" If answer is nothing for 3 days straight, simulator shows your graph with the particle stuck at the same node. It hasn't moved. Like you.

## Data & Intelligence

48. **Mentor Match** — from 140+ School of Hard Knox interviews, simulator finds the person with the most similar path to yours and tells you exactly what they did at the gate where you're stuck now.
49. **Success Fingerprint** — analyze 140+ interviews, extract the common DNA of winners. Not generic advice — the 4-5 specific micro-actions ALL of them did, in order. Compare with your current behavior: "You're missing 2 out of 5."
50. **Counter-Narrative** — for every story you tell yourself ("not the right time", "need more preparation", "not ready"), simulator finds 5 people from knowledge base who were in WORSE position and made it. Destroys excuses with data.
51. **Bottleneck Marketplace** — simulator identifies bottlenecks where most people die → suggests: "This bottleneck is a $X/year business. Here's who's already doing it and how."

## Behavioral

52. **Invisible Cost Calculator** — every day you DON'T act has a cost. Simulator calculates: "Every day you don't send Upwork proposals costs you $47 in lost future earnings." Compound interest applied to decisions.
53. **Decision Fatigue Monitor** — more decisions in a day = worse quality. Simulator tracks: "Don't decide anything important after 4pm. Your judgment is degraded by 40%."
54. **Environment Audit** — the 5 closest people determine your outcome (Jim Rohn). Enter your inner circle → simulator calculates average of their sacred roots, tells you where your environment drags you down.
55. **Scenario Autopsy** — take a past decision that went wrong. Reconstruct the graph of THEN. Simulator shows exactly at which gate you went wrong and what you should have done.
56. **Energy Budget** — each node costs energy (decisional, emotional, physical). You have a finite daily budget. Simulator shows: "You're spending 80% of energy on Simulator ($0) and 5% on Upwork (potential $5K/mo)."

## Legacy & Long-term

57. **Death Clock** — enter age, health, lifestyle. Simulator shows how many weeks you statistically have left and what you can realistically build in that time. Every simulation shows the time cost.
58. **1000 Days Map** — 1000 days from today. Simulator creates 3 parallel timelines based on 3 action levels: minimum (as now), moderate (1h/day focused), maximum (all-in). The visual difference is the shock you need.
59. **Legacy Simulator** — simulate not your life but the impact of your choices on 3 generations. "If Richard does X today, what changes for his children in 2050?" Uses real intergenerational mobility data.
60. **Inflection Point Alert** — simulator monitors your situation and alerts: "You're at a turning point. The next 2 weeks determine the next 2 years. Here's why." Based on pattern recognition from data.
