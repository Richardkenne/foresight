Human Path

Pressure
No cash buffer. The person is not exploring for fun, but trying to breathe.
Attempt
Chooses something cheap to start: services, gig work, simple offers, content.
Silence
The worst phase is no feedback: no money, no proof, rising doubt.
Proof
The first $100 does not solve life, but it breaks the feeling of impossibility.
Separation
After that, the path splits: trap, burnout, or a system that finally supports life.
