Human Path

Pressure
Starts from scarcity. This is not ambition-only; it is survival pressure.
Choice
Picks a low-capital path because there is no room for expensive mistakes.
Delay
The system becomes dangerous during the empty period before proof appears.
Proof
The first $100 matters emotionally more than financially: it says the world may respond.
Break Point
At $500 the path feels real. At $1K it starts acting less like hustle and more like system.

Evidence Notes

First $100 / early proof

1.8%
In upwork-data.json, `18 / 1000` signups earn `$100` within 90 days. Same file also gives `time_to_first_dollar_months = 1-3`, median `1.5`.

Time to first client

1-2 weeks
In time-to-result-benchmarks.json, `Freelancing (cold outreach)` takes `1-2 weeks` to first client, `local services` `1-7 days`, `productized service` `1-3 weeks`.

$500/month threshold

$500 median
In side-hustle-entrepreneurship.json, `Median monthly side hustle income = $500`, average `$891`, and `47%` earn under `$500/month`.

$1K+/month threshold

15-20%
In upwork-data.json, `15-20%` of active earners exceed `$1K/month`. In time-to-result-benchmarks.json, `Productized service -> $1K/month = 1-2 months`.

Quit / abandonment

60-70%
In upwork-data.json, `freelancer_quit_year_1_pct = 60-70%`, and `freelancer_never_earn_pct = 50-70%`.

Burnout

43% / 63%
side-hustle-entrepreneurship.json gives `43%` side hustle burnout in year 1. upwork-data.json gives `63%` burnout in the past year for freelancers.
